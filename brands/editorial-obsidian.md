# Brand Skin: Editorial Obsidian (`editorial-obsidian`)

## Archetype
High-contrast dark cinema & SaaS launch stage (inspired by Linear, Raycast, Apple Pro hardware reveals).

## Color Tokens
- **Ground (`--color-ground`):** `#0D1117` (Obsidian Slate — always paired with subtle radial glow + 35mm film grain)
- **Surface (`--color-surface`):** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.86)` with `1.5px solid rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.18)`
- **Primary Ink (`--color-ink`):** `#F8FAFC` (Crisp Alabaster, WCAG contrast > 15:1)
- **Primary Accent (`--color-accent`):** `#CCFF00` (Electric Chartreuse — max 1 hero word or primary CTA per shot)
- **Secondary Accent (`--color-cyan`):** `#38BDF8` (Telemetry / Rim Light Cyan)

## Typography
- **Display Hero:** `Outfit` (800/900, `-0.035em` tracking) or `Oswald` (800/900 uppercase)
- **Editorial Contrast:** `Playfair Display` (700 italic for emotional emphasis)
- **Technical Mono:** `JetBrains Mono` (700 uppercase, `0.08em–0.20em` letter-spacing, minimum `28px` at 1080p)

## Anti-Slop Rules
- Never use default purple/violet SaaS gradients (`#6366F1` -> `#A855F7`).
- Never use emojis as UI badges or bullet icons.
- Landed titles hold 100% dead-still during reading (`0.5s + 0.3s/word`); move the camera or background 3D stage instead.
